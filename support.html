Fold & Flick Support

Fold it. Flick it. Fly it.

How to play

Fold a paper plane, then swipe up and to the right to throw it. Hold anywhere to dive and gain speed, and let go to glide. Ride the wind up the hills, fly through the rings and reach the flag fast for three stars.

Plane codes and challenges

Share any plane as a code. Your friend opens the hangar, taps Enter a code and pastes it in. A challenge code also brings the course and your time to beat.

Daily Flight

There's a new course every day, and it's the same for everyone. Tap Share after you finish to post your result.
