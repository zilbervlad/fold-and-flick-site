Fold & Flick Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Fold & Flick is a paper airplane game. It works offline. You don't need an account, and there are no ads.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or send any personal information, and it does not track you. There are no analytics, no advertising and no third-party data sharing.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, best times, Daily Flight streak, your best-run ghosts and your planes) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

When you tap Share (a Daily Flight result, a plane code, a challenge or a replay clip), the game hands it to your device's share sheet or clipboard. You choose where it goes. We never see it.

Children

Fold & Flick is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or bugs? Contact us through the developer link on our App Store page.
